About 30 minutes

Set up your Google Business Profile

This is what makes your tile company show up on Google and Google Maps when people search for a tile setter. Do these steps in order.

  1. 1

    Get these ready

    • A Gmail account you'll keep for the business
    • Your business name, exactly as it's written on your truck and invoices
    • Your phone number
    • The towns you work in
    • Your logo and 10 to 20 photos of finished tile jobs
  2. 2

    Go to google.com/business

    Open google.com/business, tap Manage now, and sign in with your business Gmail.

  3. 3

    Type your business name

    If your business already pops up in the list, tap it and claim it. If not, keep typing to make a new one.

  4. 4

    Pick the category "Tile contractor"

  5. 5

    Say "No" to customers visiting you

    When it asks if customers can visit your location, tap No. You go to them, so this keeps your home address private.

  6. 6

    Add the towns you work in

    Type each town or area you'll travel to for jobs.

  7. 7

    Add your phone number

    Add a website too if you have one. It's fine to skip it.

  8. 8

    Record the verification video

    Google needs proof your business is real before it shows your profile. Record one video with no cuts, in daylight, showing:

    • A street sign in a town you work in
    • Your truck with your business name on it, and your tools
    • An invoice, business card or business license

    Google usually approves it in a few days. If it gets rejected, record it again more clearly.

  9. 9

    Fill in the rest

    • Services: showers, tub surrounds, backsplashes, floors, waterproofing, repairs
    • Hours
    • Photos: your logo, then your best tile jobs
    • Description: a few lines about what you do and where
    Example description[Your business] is a tile setting company serving [town], [town] and [town]. For [X] years we've installed custom showers, tub surrounds, backsplashes and tile floors for homeowners and builders. Clean layouts, proper waterproofing, and we show up when we say we will. Call for a free quote.
  10. 10

    Ask every customer for a review

    Reviews are what get you calls. In your profile, tap Ask for reviews to get your link. Text it to each happy customer the day you finish their job.

Don't do these (Google can shut your profile down)

  • Don't add extra words to your name, like "Best Tile Setter". Use your real business name.
  • Don't make more than one profile for your business.
  • Don't pay for reviews or give discounts for them.

Then add Michael so he can optimize it

Once your profile is verified, add Michael as a manager. He'll write your description and services, set up your categories and towns, add your photos and get your review link ready.

  1. Open your Business Profile on Google.
  2. Tap Settings (the three dots), then People and access.
  3. Tap Add.
  4. Type in Michael's email. He'll text it to you.
  5. Choose Manager and tap Invite.

You stay the owner. You can remove him anytime.
